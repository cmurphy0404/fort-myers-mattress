Mattress financing has gotten weird. Every retailer offers something — store cards with 24% APR, Affirm with hard credit pulls, "0% interest for 12 months" deals that suddenly hit you with backdated interest if you're a day late. Most of it is designed to look free and end up costing you.

Our financing is purposely simple. Here's how it works.

## How $20-down financing works at our showroom

You put **$20 down** at your appointment. That's it for cash up front. Then you make weekly or biweekly payments (your choice) over a fixed term — usually 90 days, 6 months, or 12 months depending on the mattress price.

- **No credit check.** No hard inquiry. Won't show up on your credit report unless you default and we have to send it to collections — same as any debt.
- **No co-signer.**
- **No bait-and-switch interest** that backdates if you're late. Late fees are flat-dollar, disclosed up front.
- **Early payoff is free.** Pay it off in two weeks and the price drops, no penalty.

The financing isn't run by us — it's a third-party lease-to-own program (Acima, Snap, Progressive — varies). They take on the credit risk, we get paid up front, and you get a mattress today without a credit check.

## What it actually costs

Lease-to-own financing isn't free. Over the full 12-month term, you'll typically pay about 1.5x the cash price. So a $1,200 mattress paid out over 12 months ends up around $1,800.

This sounds bad until you compare it to:
- **A store credit card** — minimum payments at 26-29% APR keeps you paying for 3+ years on the same mattress.
- **"0% for 12 months" deals** — miss one payment, retroactive interest at 26%+ on the original principal. Hidden trap.
- **Sleeping another year on a broken mattress** — chiropractor visits, missed work, ruined sleep.

If you can pay off in 90 days, the cost premium is much smaller (usually 10-25%). Most of our customers do.

## Who this is for

- You don't have $1,000+ liquid right now
- Your credit is mid (550-680) or worse, and you don't want a hard inquiry
- You need a mattress today, not in six months

## Who should skip financing

If you have $1,200 saved and good credit, **just pay cash**. The lease-to-own premium isn't worth it when you have other options. Affirm or Klarna with their soft-pull installments will be cheaper, and a regular credit card paid off the same month is cheapest of all.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does this affect my credit score?**
No, unless you stop paying and we send it to collections. Standard for any debt — same as if you missed payments on a phone bill.

**Can I pay it off early?**
Yes, with no penalty. Most providers give a discount for early payoff. We'll show you the math at signing.

**What happens if I miss a payment?**
A flat late fee (usually $10-25) and the next payment is rescheduled. After 60-90 days delinquent, the provider may report to collections.

**Can I get this without coming in?**
Not for a mattress this size — we need to ship from our warehouse, which means we need an address, payment, and signed lease. That all happens at the appointment.
