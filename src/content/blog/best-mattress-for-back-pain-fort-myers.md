## What kind of back pain do you have?

Three rough categories, three different mattress prescriptions:

**1. Lower-back stiffness (the most common)**
You feel it most in the morning, it loosens up after you walk around. Cause is usually a mattress that's gotten too soft and lets your hips sink, throwing your spine out of neutral alignment. Solution: a **medium-firm hybrid or a memory-foam mattress with strong edge support**. Brands we'd point you at: Tempur-Pedic, Brooklyn Bedding hybrids, Sealy Posturepedic Plus.

**2. Sciatica or hip pain**
Sharp, traveling pain. Usually one side. Caused by pressure on nerves while you sleep. Solution: a **plush memory foam or pillow-top hybrid** that lets your hips and shoulders sink without bottoming out. The Tempur-Pedic ProAdapt line and Diamond Mattress's softer hybrids are workhorses here.

**3. Upper-back / shoulder / neck pain**
Often a side-sleeper problem. Solution: **medium-soft, contouring mattress + a properly-sized pillow**. The mattress matters less than people think; the pillow matters way more than people think. We'll fit both during your appointment.

## Firmness levels, in plain English

Mattress firmness is rated 1-10. Here's what those numbers actually mean for someone in pain:

| Firmness | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| 3-4 (Plush) | Side sleepers under 150 lbs, pressure-point pain |
| 5-6 (Medium) | Most people, most of the time |
| 6-7 (Medium-firm) | Back sleepers, mild back pain, couples with mixed preferences |
| 7-8 (Firm) | Stomach sleepers, sciatica, heavier sleepers needing more lift |
| 9-10 (Extra-firm) | Specific orthopedic needs only — most people don't need this |

The "extra firm = good for back pain" idea is a myth from 1985. Real chiropractors recommend medium-firm for the majority of back pain patients. We'll let you actually lie on each option for as long as you want.

Not always. For most lower-back pain, **medium-firm** is the sweet spot. Firmer than that and you create new pressure points at the hips and shoulders.
